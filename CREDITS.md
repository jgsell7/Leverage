# Credits and third-party sources

A record of the outside material Levered uses, the license each comes under, and when it was checked.

Last reviewed: November 2, 2026

## Exercise photos and part of the exercise list

- **Source:** free-exercise-db, https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db
  (a restructured copy of the earlier public-domain dataset wrkout/exercises.json)
- **What Levered uses:** the start-and-end exercise photos in `ph/`, packed into image sheets, and part of the exercise list in the Exercise library. Levered's own exercises, coaching cues, workouts and other text are original to Levered.
- **License:** the Unlicense, which dedicates the work to the public domain. It allows copying, changing and commercial use without attribution.
- **Note:** the dataset does not say who originally took the photos. They are used in good faith under the license above. If anyone shows they hold rights to a photo, it will be removed or replaced promptly. Replacing the full set with photos Levered owns is planned.

## Food and nutrition data

- **Source:** USDA FoodData Central, National Nutrient Database (SR Legacy), U.S. Department of Agriculture, https://fdc.nal.usda.gov
- **License:** U.S. government work, in the public domain (CC0 1.0).
- **Community foods** are added by people who use Levered and are covered by the in-app report and removal process.
