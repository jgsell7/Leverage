Privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Levered is a workout app. This policy explains what information Levered keeps, where it's stored, who can see it, and how to delete it. In short: your data is used only to run the app for you. It isn't sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

What Levered stores

  • On your device. Everything you log is saved in your browser or the app on your device: workouts, sets, workout history, calendar, body weight and height, cardio, equipment, nutrition logs and goals, and settings. This works without an account.
  • Your account, if you create one. Your email address and password. Passwords are handled by our account provider and stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form; nobody, including us, can read them.
  • Synced data, if you're signed in. A copy of the information listed above is saved to your account so it can appear on your other devices.
  • Community foods you choose to share. If you share a food with the community, its name, brand, serving size and nutrition values are visible to everyone who uses Levered. Your name and email are not shown with it.
  • Feedback and reports you send. Your message, the type of feedback, an email address if you choose to give one, the screen you were on, your device type, browser, screen size and the app version. Reports about community foods include which food you reported, the reason and any details you add.

Levered doesn't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and doesn't collect your location, contacts or photos. A photo you take to scan a nutrition label, where that feature is offered, is used only to read the label and isn't kept.

Health and fitness information

Workout, body and nutrition information is personal. It's used only to show you your own history, trends and suggestions. It isn't used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone. Levered's suggestions are general fitness information, not medical advice.

Who can see your data

  • Your synced data is protected so that only your signed-in account can read or change it. Other people using Levered can't see it.
  • Feedback and reports can be read only by the person who runs Levered, to fix problems and review community foods.
  • Community foods you share are public within Levered, as described above.

Services that help run Levered

  • Supabase stores accounts, synced data, community foods, feedback and reports.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the web version of the app.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the app's fonts and a code library.

Like most web services, these providers can see technical information such as your IP address and browser when the app loads. They process it under their own privacy policies and only to provide their services.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account in the app. Tap the account button at the top (it says Sign in or Synced), then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, everything synced to it, and any foods you shared. It also clears Levered's data from that device.
  • Without an account, your data lives only on your device. Clearing your browser's data for Levered, or removing the app, deletes it.
  • Feedback you sent can be deleted on request through Help & support.

Keeping your data

Synced data is kept for as long as you have an account and is removed when you delete it. Feedback and reports are kept only as long as they're useful for improving the app.

Children

Levered isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email leverage.backendsupport@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy or your data, or want something deleted? Email leverage.backendsupport@gmail.com, use the Feedback button in the app, or see Help & support.
