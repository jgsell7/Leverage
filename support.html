Help & support

Answers to common questions. Can't find yours? Get in touch at the bottom of this page.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything you log saves on your device right away. An account backs up your data and syncs it to your other devices. To create one, tap Sign in at the top of the app, then Create an account.

How does syncing work?

When you're signed in, changes save to your account a moment after you make them, and the app picks up changes from your other devices whenever you open it or switch back to it. The button at the top shows Synced when everything is saved. If it says Sync issue, you're probably offline; it keeps trying on its own.

I forgot my password

Email leverage.backendsupport@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, or use the Feedback button in the app (choose Something's broken) and include that email. We'll help you get back in.

How do I move my data to a new device or app version?

The easiest way is to sign in with the same account on the new device. You can also go to Settings → Your data → Save backup, then open Levered on the other device and use Load backup. Loading keeps the workouts already there and adds the ones from the file.

A community food has wrong or inappropriate information

Open the food in Nutrition and tap Report. It's hidden for you straight away, and reported foods are reviewed and fixed or removed for everyone.

How do I delete my account?

Tap the account button at the top of the app (it says Sign in or Synced), then Delete account. This permanently removes your account, everything synced to it, and any foods you shared. See the privacy policy for details.

Is Levered medical advice?

No. Levered's suggestions are general fitness information. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training or nutrition plan, especially if you have an injury or health condition.

Contact
